Cortex - AI layer over ERP data - Platform in homologation
Anthropic Claude API, Third-party ERP integration, Incremental synchronisation, Multi-company
2026
Problem: administrative work across departments, such as chasing receivables, answering stock

Constraint: the data sits in Deltaze, the commercial ERP the company runs on. No privileged access
